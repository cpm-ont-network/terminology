Abstract

The Construction Terminology (CTERM) Ontology provides controlled terminology for describing construction-related entities, resources, activities, works, cost components, parameters, classifications, materials, functions, uses, dimensions, performances, and other technical characteristics. CTERM is designed as a shared terminology layer that can be reused by the Cost Item (CI) and Construction Resources (CR) ontologies.

Introduction back to ToC

Construction information is frequently expressed through heterogeneous terminology, synonyms, and domain-specific descriptions, limiting semantic interoperability and automated data processing. The Construction Terminology (CTERM) Ontology provides a shared controlled vocabulary for describing construction concepts and technical attributes in a consistent and machine-readable way. It supports the semantic description of cost items and construction resources while remaining independent from the application-specific structures of CI and CR.
